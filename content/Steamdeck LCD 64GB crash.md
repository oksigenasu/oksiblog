Steamdeck lcd 64GB always crash  
  
Chronology:  
the owner wants to update this steamdeck from the stock 32GB SSD to 64GB by themselves. but after breaking the SDcard inside when opening (whoops) and causing instability in the system, they decided to sell this device at cheap citing SSD connector problem or something.  
  
so we bought it.  
  
after checking for a while, the system was indeed unstable. couple of crash here and there, but nothing physical. after failed to restore with recovery OS, its time to open this bad boy.  
  
surprisingly this device is super lightweight. but the back cover feel solid before sliding our nail to those tabs. no wonder the owner forgot the SDcard and Kiko-ing it.  
  
after removing the motherboard, pulling out the remnant of the SDcard, and inspecting the haardware, we found out that we have a missing screw on the motherboard side near the SSD (sign of a seasoned technician).  
  
welp, since there are no visible damage, lets just reseat the SSD, moving the missing screw to the fan, and proceed to reinstall the recovery disk.  
  
reinstalling the recovery is simple, all we need to do is google the instructions, be done with it and voila! a steam interface.  
  
after making sure the system is functioning properly, its already sold again within 3 days...  
  
yap, profit.
