This week patient's are:  
Macbook Pro 13 Inch 2017 touchbar edition with a little bit of heat and screen problem.  
  
this is the first time i opened a macbook laptop. using guides from various sites and videos, i was successfull in opening the back cover this laptop. at a glance, the insides looks pretty clean, but we have no idea the condition how the thermal paste under the heatsink.  
  
further disassembly is needed to access the heatsink and thermal paste. after unscrewing a ton of screws and releasing dozen of flex cables, finally the motherboard can be released.  
  
unscrewing another 4 screws and releasing a tiny flex cable, the fan can be taken out. cleaning both fan using paintbrush, releases a lot of micro dust that accumulated on the fan's fin. after it looks clean, now its time to repaste.  
  
to release the heatsink, we need to access 4 screws under the motherboard. after releasing the heatsink we can see that the thermal paste is quite dry. after cleaning the old paste, and applying a 75K Cheap Deepcool paste, we reassemble the heatsink carefully.  
  
after reassembling the motherboard with all the screws and flex cables, we close the under cover and try to turn it on. pressing the fingerprint button, the macbook start slowly and finally logged in.  
  
opening the terminal and running the temperature check, it shows improvement from 60 degree centigrade before to 50-ish.  
  
fyuuh job done. turns out the macbook is not that complicated (even though it has a lot of screws and cables). the position of the screw and the cables are easy to remember and the flow is quite easy to remember.  
  
maybe this is the first time i reassemble a laptop without a leftover screw.
