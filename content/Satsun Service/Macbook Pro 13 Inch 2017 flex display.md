another problem this macbook has is the screen sometimes loses the black color (it turns to gray-ish) when the screen is opened at certain angle. at first the owner and i thought this was the infamous "flexgate". where the backlight cable is failing because of the tight constraint when its flex (hence flexgate).  
  
but after opening and examining the backlight cable, it looks mint. and rigging the cable back and forth shows no effect on the problem, we conclude that this is not a flexgate. after more digging on google and found Louis Rossman video about dustgate, we hope that cleaning the display cable would fix the problem.  
  
after rippingout the screen and checking the display cable under the screen using magnifier, we can see a tiny dent on the flex cable courtesy of accumulated dust and flex pressure. display dusted because of a speck of dust? unbelievable right?  
  
cleaning the pastic under the flex cable using tissue and scraping the corner where the plastic and flex cable met using paper we can finally remove most of the accumulated dust. finger cross this gonna works.  
  
after putting back the screen and the wifi antenna, we can finally reassemble the laptop and see the effect.  
  
lo and behold! black color! unbelievable! really!? speck of dust!? this is the first time i have fixed screen problem just using paper and tissue!  
  
i am out of words....
