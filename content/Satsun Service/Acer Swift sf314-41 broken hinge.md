Todays patient are:  
Acer Swift sf314-41  
  
its common for this slim type of laptop to have broken hinge. with slim metal case and huge lcd screen, this assembly weigh quite a bit and with a "little" accident its quite common for the assembly to break. even though the case is metal, the thread for the screw still nested in plastic. thats why once its break, sometimes the threaded insert gone along with it.  
  
another thing that made this kind of laptop so fragile is the hinge's fulcrum. you might have heard that a good laptop can be opened with one hand. thats because the hinge is calibrated so that it is stiff enough to hold the screen weight but light enough to be lifted with one hand.  
  
on this kind of laptop, usually the hinge is so stiff that even with two hands, you would still have a hard time opening it. and after a while, you will have a broken hinge with ripped thread insert. enough with the theory.  
  
after opening the screen bezel cover we can see clearly the plastic where the threaded insert used to be. finding replacement insert and the appropriate screw would be pain and taking too long.  
  
since the owner need the fix asap, the fastest way to fix this is by "fixing" the hinge using adhesive and weld it down to the case using infamous tiktok tricks: fine dust and cyanoacrylate glue. some people use cigarette ash or baking soda, some even use cigarette butt.  
  
to fix the hinge to the bezel we use polychloroprene glue. its a magic glue that sticks to anything from stone to plastic as long as you use it correctly. apply the glue to the hinge and the case evenly and wait for around 15 minute to dry before sticking it together and apply pressure.  
  
after that we support the hinge using CA glue and facial cotton. put the cotton inside the threaded insert hole then a drop of CA glue and build up the layers with more cotton. do it for the other hole and the hinge is solid as a rock.  
  
to prevent the accident to happen again, we tune the hinge fulcrum by loosen and tighten the nut on the end of the fulcrum. after a while we finally have a hinge that can be opened with one hand.
